# Care Navigation Advocates

Care Navigation Advocates is a team of physicians and healthcare providers building **Care**, a health navigation app for patients and the people who care for them. Care helps people understand their symptoms, lab results, medications, and insurance in plain language, and connects them with real doctors and care agents when they need a human. For clinics and care managers, a provider dashboard supports referrals, follow-ups, and care coordination.

Our mission is to give everyone clear, confident, and accessible guidance in navigating their health, grounded in real clinical experience.
